# 📊 AtliQ Hardwares – Sales & Financial Performance Analysis

> **An end-to-end Business Intelligence project focused on Sales, Customer, Market and Profitability Analysis using Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot & DAX.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales and financial performance of **AtliQ Hardwares** across different years, customers, markets and months.

The objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can help understand:

* 📈 Sales growth and performance
* 👥 Customer-wise sales contribution
* 🌎 Market-wise performance
* 🎯 Actual sales vs target
* 💰 Gross Margin and GM%
* 📅 Monthly and fiscal-year trends
* 📊 Year-over-year performance

## 📑 Reports Included

### 1️⃣ Customer Performance Report

Analyzes customer-level Net Sales performance across 2019, 2020 and 2021.

Key metrics include:

* Net Sales by Customer
* Year-over-Year Growth
* 2021 vs 2020 performance
* Customer growth comparison

## Overall Net Sales increased from **87.5M in 2019 → 196.7M in 2020 → 598.9M in 2021**, representing **304.5% growth from 2020 to 2021**.

### 2️⃣ Market Performance vs Target

This report compares actual 2021 sales against the target for different countries.

Key metrics:

* 2021 Net Sales
* Target
* Target Variance
* Performance %

## The overall 2021 sales were **598.9M**, while the target variance was **-54.9M**, resulting in overall performance of **-9.17% vs target**.

### 3️⃣ P&L Statement – Fiscal Year

This report provides a year-wise view of the Profit & Loss statement.

| Metric       |  2019 |   2020 |   2021 |
| ------------ | ----: | -----: | -----: |
| Net Sales    | 87.5M | 196.7M | 598.9M |
| COGS         | 51.2M | 123.4M | 380.7M |
| Gross Margin | 36.2M |  73.3M | 218.2M |
| GM %         | 41.4% |  37.3% |  36.4% |

Net Sales grew strongly in 2021, while GM% decreased from **37.3% to 36.4%** compared with 2020.

---

### 4️⃣ P&L Statement – Market Wise

The market-level P&L report analyzes:

* Net Sales
* COGS
* Gross Margin
* Gross Margin %

for each market in FY2021.

## For example, **India generated 161.26M in Net Sales with a 32.0% GM%**, while the USA generated **87.78M with a 37.0% GM%**.

### 5️⃣ P&L Statement – Monthly Analysis

The monthly P&L report provides a detailed view of:

* Monthly Net Sales
* COGS
* Gross Margin
* GM%
* Year-over-Year comparison

For FY2021, monthly Net Sales ranged from **41.5M to 78.1M** in the reported period, allowing monthly performance trends to be analyzed.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

### 🚀 Strong Sales Growth

AtliQ Hardwares experienced significant growth:

**87.5M → 196.7M → 598.9M**

from 2019 to 2021.

### 🎯 Target Gap

Despite strong sales growth, the company was **9.17% below the 2021 target** overall.

### 💰 Gross Margin

Gross Margin increased from **36.2M in 2019 to 218.2M in 2021**, while GM% moved from **41.4% to 36.4%**.

### 🌎 Market Performance

The analysis shows significant differences in sales and profitability across markets, making it possible to identify high-revenue and high-margin markets.
